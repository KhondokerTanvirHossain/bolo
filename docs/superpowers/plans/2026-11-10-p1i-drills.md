# P1i Real Drills Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal (DRL-1…7, US-5.1…5.7):** "Practise (3 min)" opens up to 3 drills made from that talk's fixes:
- **minimal pairs:** 3 listen rounds, then 5 say rounds;
- **shadowing:** hear the model, the mic opens, hear both back, with 0.75×;
- **say it better:** read, then key words hidden, then from memory.

All drills have Skip, which queues the drill for next time and retires it after 3 skips. Each item allows 3 tries, then "Good effort. We'll come back to this one." The run ends with a summary of what moved and one **Done**.

**Architecture:**
- **coaching module:** `GET /api/talk/{id}/drills` builds the drills from the debrief's fixes with a pure `DrillPlan`. A sound fix's pairs and say words come from the assessment module's new port in, `MinimalPairsUseCase`, backed by a `MinimalPairs` deck next to `PracticeDeck`.
- **Model audio:** `GET /api/talk/{id}/drills/say?text=…` speaks only texts that are part of that talk's drills, in the Coach's voice. It's cached at `tts/coach/<sha256>.wav`, shared, and not personal data.
- **Scoring:** every spoken attempt uses the existing `POST /api/attempts` (scripted assessment; `mode=upgrade` for say it better), so attempts also count as active days.
- **web:** a `DrillSession` view with `PairsDrill`, `ShadowDrill`, `SayBetterDrill` and `DrillDone`. The debrief's "Practise (3 min)", History's "Practise these (3 min)" and Home's practice card open it. "Practise sounds" stays the free sound library.

## Global Constraints
- **Drill per fix, in fix order, at most 3** (R5):
  - a sound fix → **pairs** (its pattern);
  - the first upgrade → **sayBetter** (said, better, the changed words);
  - a second upgrade → **shadow** (its better sentence);
  - the short-answers habit → **shadow** (its model answer);
  - the filler habit → no drill (a habit for talks). This is ruled.
  - Disputed fixes are already gone from the body, so they make no drill.
- **Pairs deck** (real words; teacher review later):
  - V_BH: very/berry, vest/best, van/ban; say very, voice, video, seven, live.
  - W_V: wet/vet, wine/vine, west/vest; say we, water, work, window, away.
  - TH_TD: three/tree, thin/tin, path/pat; say think, three, thank, bath, mouth.
  - Z_J: zest/jest, zig/jig, buzz/budge; say zoo, zero, easy, busy, prize.
  - S_SH: sip/ship, see/she, sell/shell; say she, shop, fish, wash, sure.
  - S_CLUSTER_VOWEL: steam/esteem, state/estate, scape/escape; say school, speak, street, study, smile.
  - In listen rounds the target is the pattern's sound word (very, wet, three, zest, ship, steam), and the learner picks which word they heard. A miss plays both words again, slowly (0.75×).
- **Tries:** 3 per item (a say word, a shadow, a say-better round). After the third try below Nailed it: "Good effort. We'll come back to this one." and it moves on by itself after 1.5 s.
- **Results:** Nailed it, Closer or Once more (the existing levels). A listen round is right or "Listen again".
- **Skip:**
  - always available in the header, along with **Close** (Esc).
  - A skipped drill goes to this browser's queue (`bolo.drillQueue`, by drill key) and comes first next time, within the 3.
  - After 3 skips it's retired. This is ruled: a browser queue, not a server one.
- **Header:** close, three dots (the drill count and where you are) and Skip. The Coach mark shows, since drills are the Coach's.
- **One filled button per screen** (R1).
- **Summary (DRL-7):** one line per drill, before → after, for example "The v sound: Closer → Nailed it" (first vs best say result); "Say it better: Once more → Closer"; skipped: "Skipped. It comes back next time." Nothing that failed. One **Done**, which goes Home.
- **The say endpoint** refuses any text that isn't in that talk's drills (404). A talk that isn't yours is 404. At most 30 synthesis requests a minute per learner (429).

## Review Focus
1. The say endpoint can't be used as a free text-to-speech service: other texts are 404, other learners' talks are 404, and there's a rate limit. Test: Task 1.
2. A debrief with no fixes, or only a filler habit, has no drills; the web says so and offers Practise sounds. Test: Task 1 and Task 2.
3. A recording deleted or swept: the shadow's model comes from the say endpoint, not the deleted model clip. Test: Task 1 `shadowUsesTheSayEndpoint`.
4. Closing midway (Esc) keeps the completed drills' results, and the rest go to the queue. Test: Task 2 e2e.
5. 390 px: the drill cards fit, and the pairs buttons stay side by side. Test: Task 2 e2e.
