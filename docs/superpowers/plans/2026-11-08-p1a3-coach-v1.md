# P1a-3 Coach v1 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** When a talk ends, the learner waits behind "Your coach is listening back" for at most ~15 s. Then the Coach's debrief opens:
- 1 specific strength, with the learner's clip;
- up to 3 fix cards, each with the learner's clip, a model in the Coach's voice and a 1-line tip;
- the 4 skill levels;
- "That's not what I said" removes a card.

Too little speech gives the honest "not enough talking" state.

**Architecture:**
- **Live scoring:** the live recogniser runs unscripted pronunciation assessment (Azure, `en-US`, phoneme level, IPA), so every learner segment arrives already scored, with no second request. That matters because F0 allows one live recognition.
- **Turn records:** the relay keeps each learner turn's audio (encrypted on disk) and its scored words.
- **At talk end:** a coaching job runs.
  - Pure `assessment` domain code turns the scored words and transcript into sound-pattern candidates, fluency stats and levels.
  - One Claude call with a JSON schema writes the strength and the "one step better" sentence upgrades.
  - Azure TTS (Coach voice) renders the models, and clips are cut from the learner's audio.
  - The result is stored as a debrief row.
- **Web:** polls the debrief and renders the prototype's `scoring` and `debrief` screens.

**Tech Stack:** Azure Speech SDK 1.53 `PronunciationAssessmentConfig`; `claude-sonnet-5-5` with structured output (`output_config.format`); AES-256-GCM (`javax.crypto`) for audio at rest; Next.js.

**Spec:** `Bolo-PRD-MVP.md` §5.3 ASM-1/2/3/5/6/7, §5.4 DBF-1/2/3/4/5, US-3.1–3.7, US-4.1–4.7, NFR-13/14/16; `Bolo-Architecture-MVP.md` §3.1 (`assessment`, `coaching`, `media`), §5. Prototype: `design/prototypes/desktop/index.html` screens `scoring` and `debrief`. Live finding (2026-11-08): en-IN assessment returns phonemes with empty symbols, so pattern detection needs `en-US`.

## Global Constraints
- Java owns every rule (ranking, thresholds, levels); the web renders the debrief JSON.
- **ASM-5 confidence first.** v1 shows **at most 1 sound fix**, and only for a pattern seen **≥ 2 times** in the session, each with phoneme accuracy < 50 inside a word with accuracy < 75. Every other fix comes from the transcript (sentence upgrade, habit).
- ASM-6: under 60 s of learner speech gives the `too_short` state ("Not enough talking to give you useful feedback yet" / "Talk a bit more").
- DBF-1 strength cites a real moment, never generic praise. US-4.4 upgrades keep the meaning, stay ≤ 25 words, and go one step better.
- Copy rules (design-system voice): no banned words in learner-facing text (wrong, bad, error, mistake, fail, incorrect, poor, phoneme, fricative…). Name sounds as "The v sound · v as in *van*".
- NFR-13: audio is encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM, key `BOLO_MEDIA_KEY`, 32 bytes base64). NFR-14: with consent "No" (keepRecordings=false), audio is deleted 24 h after the talk; otherwise after 90 days.
- Logs never carry transcript text or audio.
- Bolo's voice and persona don't change (founder discussion pending). The Coach has its own voice: `en-US-AndrewNeural`, config `bolo.coaching.voice`.
- Audio is served only to its owner (session learner) via `/api/media/...`.

## Review Focus
1. A talk that ends by drop, time-up or End still gets a debrief. One that ends `voice_unavailable` before any learner turn gets none, and the web shows its normal message.
2. Claude returns a quote that isn't in the transcript, or an upgrade over 25 words: that item is dropped, not shown.
3. A turn whose audio or assessment is missing (recogniser hiccup): the debrief is still built from what exists; clips fall back to the whole turn (capped at 12 s) or are omitted.
4. Another learner's session id on `/api/talk/{id}/debrief` or `/api/media/...` gets 404, never their audio.
5. The scoring job fails (LLM error): 2 retries, then `failed` with "We couldn't prepare your feedback. Your talk is saved." No infinite spinner.

### Task 3: Assessment domain (pure)

**Files:**
- Create in `assessment/domain/`:
  - `SoundPattern` (enum: `V_BH("v")`, `W_V("w")`, `TH_TD("θ","ð")`, `Z_J("z")`, `S_SH("ʃ")`, each with a learner name, an example word and a mouth tip);
  - `TurnScore` (record: idx, text, words, durationMs);
  - `PatternDetector`;
  - `FluencyStats`;
  - `SkillLevels`;
  - `DebriefPlan`.
- `PatternDetector.candidates(List<TurnScore>)` → `List<SoundIssue(pattern, count, examples: List<WordAt(turnIdx, word, startMs, endMs)>)>`.
  - It counts a hit when a phoneme of the pattern scores < 50 inside a word with accuracy < 75.
  - It keeps patterns with count ≥ 2, ordered by count.
- `FluencyStats.of(turns)`:
  - speech seconds (sum of first-word-start → last-word-end per turn, else text length × 0.35 s);
  - words per minute;
  - pauses > 1.2 s between words inside a turn;
  - fillers, counted from text: um, uh, like, actually, basically, "you know";
  - average words per learner turn.
- `SkillLevels`, with levels `BUILDING/GOOD/STRONG`:
  - pronunciation, from mean word accuracy (≥ 85 STRONG, ≥ 70 GOOD);
  - fluency, from wpm (≥ 110 and ≤ 2 long pauses/min → STRONG; ≥ 80 → GOOD);
  - sentence building and vocabulary, taken from the coach call.
- `DebriefPlan.choose(soundIssues, upgrades, habit)` → ordered fixes, max 3:
  - at most 1 sound fix (v1);
  - at least 1 upgrade or habit when one exists;
  - sound first, then upgrades, then habit.
- Habit rule:
  - fillers ≥ 5 → filler habit (the most used filler and its count);
  - otherwise an average answer under 8 words → short-answers habit;
  - otherwise none.
- Tests (in `assessment/domain/*Test.java`):
  - detector: two low-`v` words → V_BH ×2; one → none; a low `v` in a word with accuracy 90 → none;
  - fluency numbers on a hand-built turn;
  - levels thresholds;
  - plan: 2 sound issues + 2 upgrades + habit → [sound, upgrade, upgrade]; no upgrades → [sound, habit];
  - too short: 59 s of speech → `tooShort()`.
- [ ] RED, GREEN, full suite; commit `feat(api): assessment domain for sound patterns, fluency and levels`.

### Task 4: The Coach (coaching module + API)

**Files:**
- Create in `coaching`:
  - `application/port/in/DebriefUseCase.java` (`void composeAsync(TalkSessionId)`, `Optional<DebriefView> view(LearnerId, TalkSessionId)`, `void dispute(LearnerId, TalkSessionId, int fixIndex)`);
  - `application/service/CoachService.java`;
  - `application/port/out/DebriefPort.java`, `adapter/out/persistence/JdbcDebriefAdapter.java`;
  - `domain/CoachPrompt.java`;
  - `adapter/in/web/DebriefController.java` (`GET /api/talk/{id}/debrief`, `POST /api/talk/{id}/debrief/fixes/{n}/dispute`, `GET /api/media/talk/{id}/{file}`).
- `talk` calls `DebriefUseCase.composeAsync` from `TalkConversation.end` when the talk has ≥ 1 learner turn.
- `LlmRequest` gains an optional `jsonSchema` (Map); the Anthropic adapter sends it as `output_config.format` (JSON schema) and `effort: low`.
- The coach call:
  - model `bolo.coaching.model: claude-sonnet-5-5`;
  - input: the learner turns, numbered, plus Bolo's lines;
  - JSON schema: `{strength:{turn:int, quote:string, text:string}, upgrades:[{turn:int, said:string, better:string}] (max 2), sentenceLevel, vocabularyLevel ∈ BUILDING|GOOD|STRONG}`.
- Validation (Review Focus 2):
  - a quote or `said` must appear in that turn's text (case- and punctuation-insensitive contains);
  - `better` ≤ 25 words and different from `said`;
  - failures are dropped.
  - A strength that fails validation falls back to a factual line from the stats: "You talked for N minutes and took M turns."
- Clips:
  - learner clips are cut from the turn WAV using word times (`stream ms − stream_start_ms`), ±300 ms for words, the quote span for upgrades and strengths, capped at 12 s, and stored as `talk/{id}/clip-{n}.wav`;
  - model clips use Coach-voice TTS (24 kHz WAV) of: the learner's sentence containing the word, said properly (sound fix); `better` (upgrade); a 3-part pattern line (short-answers habit).
- Body JSON for the web:
  - `{status, strength:{text, clip}`;
  - `fixes:[{kind: sound|upgrade|habit, title, said, saidHighlight, better, betterHighlight, tip, clip, model, count, word}]`;
  - `skills:{pronunciation, fluency, sentenceBuilding, vocabulary}`;
  - `firstSession, latency:{p50Ms, p90Ms}}`.
- Retries: 2 automatic retries with 2 s backoff, then `failed`. At startup, `scoring` rows older than 2 min become `failed`.
- Disputes remove the card from the body and log `fix_dispute`.
- Tests (`CoachAcceptanceTest`, with fakes):
  - a 3-turn talk with injected low-v scores in 2 turns, plus `FakeLlm` returning a valid coach JSON → debrief `ready` within 10 s, with a strength, 1 sound fix (V_BH) + 2 upgrades, clips fetchable (WAV header);
  - an invented quote is dropped;
  - another learner gets 404 on the debrief and the media;
  - under 60 s of speech → `too_short`;
  - the LLM fails 3 times → `failed`;
  - a dispute removes the card and stores a row.
  - Test speech length: a `bolo.coaching.min-speech-ms` property (default 60000) that tests shorten.
- [ ] RED, GREEN, full suite; commit `feat(api): Coach v1 debrief - strength, fixes, clips, levels`.
