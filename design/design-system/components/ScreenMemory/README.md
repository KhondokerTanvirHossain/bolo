# ScreenMemory

Me → **What Bolo remembers** (2026-11-13, "Bolo knows you"; TLK-11). Bolo's short notes about the learner, kept so each talk can open with a personal follow-up, like a friend would.

- **Order:** To follow up first, because those are what Bolo will ask about. Then About you, then Your interests. Recent topics are kept too, but not shown: they only stop Bolo repeating itself.
- **To follow up:** each line has a small kind label (Plan, On your mind, Your practice) and the date the learner said it. Finished plans leave the list when the note is next updated. Threads fall away 30 days after they were last mentioned.
- **Remove (×):** removes that line at once, with no confirm (it's cheap to lose and could come back if the learner mentions it again). The 48px target sits at the row's end. Screen readers hear "Remove: <the line>".
- **Let Bolo remember our talks:** on by default. Turning it off deletes the note and stops new notes, and talks then open from the setup answers only. Turning it back on starts an empty note.
- **Forget everything Bolo knows:** quiet-neutral, not critical (critical is reserved for Delete everything). It asks once ("Bolo will forget everything it knows about you. Talks still work as before.") and empties the note, while remembering stays on.
- **Copy:** plain and kind. Lines are written the way the learner would say them, with no labels like "user prefers". Never shown: health, religion, politics, money amounts, phone numbers or addresses, or anything from a talk that ended in crisis care.
- **Empty state:** ScreenMemoryEmpty.
