# ScreenVoicePicker

Settings → **Bolo's voice** (added 2026-11-13, founder: "male or female, British, US, Indian"). The learner picks who Bolo sounds like.

- **Female / Male:** a radio group of two chips at the top.
- **English:** British, American or Indian. Each row has a radio and a **Hear it** button.
  - Hear it plays a short sample in that voice: "Hi, I'm Bolo. What would you like to talk about today?"
  - The sample always plays at Normal speed.
  - While it plays, the button reads "Playing", with a level icon. Tapping it again stops it.
  - Hearing a voice doesn't choose it.
- **Choosing:** applies at once with no Save button, from the next talk. A talk in progress keeps its voice.
- **The default** is British, Female (today's voice).
- **The footnote** says the Coach keeps its own voice and that how Bolo understands you doesn't change. No Bolo voice is the Coach's voice.
- **Accessibility:**
  - each Hear it button is labelled with its voice ("Hear the British female voice");
  - the two radio groups are named "Voice" and "Accent".
