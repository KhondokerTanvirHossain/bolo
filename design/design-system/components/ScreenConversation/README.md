# ScreenConversation

The phone talk as a live chat (TLK-8 and TLK-16, changed 2026-11-12). The scene is unchanged, and the chat sits in the empty sky between the top bar and the sun, so Bolo and the figure stay visible. Settings → Talk view → Classic brings back the earlier screen (ScreenConversationClassic).

- **Shown here:** Bolo is speaking. Its line is revealed word by word in its bubble, with Repeat; the level meter rests; time left reads "3:42 left" at the top.
- **On your turn:** your words appear live in a dashed bubble on the right (TalkChat), the meter moves with your voice, and the mic ring turns sun.
- **Size:** the chat is about 236px tall on a 760px-high phone. Bolo's bubbles stay at 18px or more and yours at 17px. Older turns fade at the top; scroll up to read them all.
- **Time left:** numbers in the top bar. The last minute reads "1 minute left", and after time is up "Wrapping up", with no colour change and no sound. The mic ring has no time arc in this view.
