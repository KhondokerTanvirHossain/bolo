# ScreenTeacherTalk

The English teacher hat in a talk (2026-11-16, founder: "my English teacher — most important"). *New in v45.* It is ScreenConversation with a teaching moment.

- **Label:** the time pill reads "English teacher".
- **When Bolo teaches:** only when a sentence can clearly be said better — never the first turn, never two in a row, at most 4 a talk. Bolo says "A smoother way to say that: "…". Try saying it." Otherwise it just replies and asks a question.
- **Say it better card** (HintCard style, `school` icon): the better sentence with the changed words in `bo-better`. It stays while the learner tries.
- **The judgement:** the learner's next turn is the retry. The ResultPill shows **Nailed it**, **Closer** or **Good try**, and Bolo says a few warm words, then carries on. The card leaves with Bolo's next question.
- With I stammer on, Bolo never teaches because of repeats, blocks or pauses, and never judges them.

**Don't:** say mistake, wrong, error or incorrect; teach more than one sentence at a time; show a score.
