# ScreenConversationRemembers

A talk that opens from what Bolo remembers (TLK-11, 2026-11-13). This is the chat view (ScreenConversation) with real content; nothing new on screen.

- **The first line:** a short greeting plus **one** personal follow-up, picked on the server:
  1. a plan that's due;
  2. then something on the learner's mind;
  3. then their practice.
  - A follow-up that opened a talk rests for the next 2 talks and 3 days.
- **Bolo reacts first, then asks:** "Wow, that sounds like a big moment", then one question. It sounds like a friend, not an interviewer. It may share a small opinion or feeling, never a made-up life of its own.
- **Then interesting, not routine:** stories, opinions, small what-ifs and choices, plus light rehearsal for the learner's goal. Never "what did you eat today" or the weather unless the learner brings it up.
- **The learner steers.** If they change the subject, Bolo follows and doesn't pull back.
- **First talk, or remembering off:** Bolo opens from the setup answers with a real question about the learner's situation (for interviews: "What kind of job are you going for?").
