# ScreenAnswerTime

Settings → **Time to answer** (AX-7, changed 2026-11-14 by the founder; replaces the "More time to answer" switch). People differ in thinking time: some want Bolo to reply the moment they stop, others need 10–30 seconds before they start.

- **Four choices**, built like ScreenMoodPicker (radio cards with one line each):
  - **Automatic** (default): Bolo times how long you take to start each answer and, after every talk, picks Quick, Normal or Relaxed from your last 30 answers (median under 3 s → Quick, up to 6 s → Normal, over 6 s → Relaxed). The first talk runs at Normal. The line shows the current pick: "Right now: Relaxed".
  - **Quick:** help at 5 s of silence, starter card at 10 s, pause at 30 s; your turn ends 1.5 s after you stop.
  - **Normal:** 8 s, 15 s, 45 s; 2.5 s (Bolo before this change).
  - **Relaxed:** 30 s, 45 s, 2 min; 3.5 s.
- **Never off:** if you stay quiet, Bolo still helps and then pauses the talk.
- A speed you choose stays until you change it; Automatic never overrides it.
- **Choosing** saves at once, from the next talk. A failed save puts the earlier choice back: "That didn't save. Try again."
- **Desktop** (Me page, Speaking): the same four options inline as a radio group named "Time to answer", with the I stammer switch below.
