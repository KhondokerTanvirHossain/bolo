# ScreenIeltsTalk

IELTS Speaking practice (2026-11-16). *New in v45.* It is ScreenConversation with Bolo as a calm examiner.

- **Label:** the time pill reads "IELTS practice".
- **Parts:** Part 1 (familiar questions), Part 2 (a cue card read aloud and shown in the chat; the learner speaks for up to 2 minutes), Part 3 (discussion). A 5-minute talk has a short Part 1 and Part 2; a 10-minute talk has all three.
- **Pace:** at least the Relaxed answering time, so thinking and the long Part 2 turn aren't cut off.
- **No band score**, ever, during or after. The usual debrief follows.

**Don't:** give feedback during the test, estimate a band, or say "you would score".
