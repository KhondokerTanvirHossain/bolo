# ScreenMoodPicker

Settings → **Bolo's mood** (added 2026-11-13). How Bolo talks: the words and manner, not the voice. It works with every voice.

- **Four moods.** Each row has a one-line description and an example opening line (quoted, with a left rule that turns sun-coloured when selected):
  - **Friendly** (default): warm and curious, like a good friend.
  - **Funny:** playful, with light jokes. Laughs with you, never at you, and never at your English.
  - **Professional:** like a friendly colleague. A bit more formal, and good for interviews and meetings. It's a tone, not a role-play or a test.
  - **Calm:** gentle and patient, with simple words. No rush. It doesn't slow the voice; Bolo's speed does that.
- **Every mood keeps Bolo's rules:**
  - never correct;
  - one question at a time;
  - safety and crisis care;
  - the "Bolo knows you" openers.
- **Choosing:** applies at once from the next talk.
- **Later:** a fifth mood (romantic is the founder's to decide) is one more row.
