# ScreenStandupTalk

A Standup rehearsal on the phone (SCN-2, US-7.3, US-7.4). *New in v44, 2026-11-14.* It is ScreenConversation with three differences; everything else (chat, time left, Pause, Done, End) is the same.

- **Label:** the time-left pill names the rehearsal: "Standup · Realistic" or "Standup · Friendly" (Free talk shows "Free talk").
- **Bolo is the manager.** It opens by asking for the update (yesterday, today, blockers) and follows up like a manager: the ETA, who's waiting, what you need. Warm, never a reprimand, never a correction.
- **Interruption (Realistic only):** after about 20 s of your speaking, Bolo cuts in at your next breath with one short question ("Sorry, quick one: when will the refund report be ready?"). Your bubble keeps what you said so far. It is an ordinary Bolo turn: no special colour, no sound. At most twice a talk, never in the first turn or the last 30 s, and never with I stammer on.
- **Freeze after an interruption:** at the learner's usual hint time (Time to answer; 15 s at Normal) the HintCard rises with standup starters ("Good question. I expect it by…", "It depends on…"). When you've answered, Bolo hands back: "Got it, carry on."

**Don't:** show a checklist of yesterday/today/blockers, score the update during the talk, or make the interruption look like an error.
