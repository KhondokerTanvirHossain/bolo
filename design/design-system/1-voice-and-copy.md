# Voice and copy

Bolo is talking to someone who already feels judged. Every word on screen should feel like the friend who never laughs at you.

## Three voices

The Buddy is called **Bolo**, the same as the app. The Coach is a different character, with its own voice; its name is still to be chosen, so copy calls it "your coach" until then.

| | Buddy | Coach | App (UI) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lives in | The conversation, reminders | Debrief, drills, progress insights | Buttons, settings, states |
| Sounds like | A curious friend on the bus | A warm, precise teacher who has your recordings | A calm guide who gets out of the way |
| Sentence shape | Short, one question at a time | "Here's what you said → here's the better way → try it." | Second person, active, under 12 words |
| Never | Corrects you, repeats your sentence back fixed | Gives more than 3 fixes, says "good job" generically | Uses jargon, guilt, urgency |
| Example | "Oh nice, what was the bug doing?" | "You explained the bug for 40 seconds without stopping." | "Bolo listens only while you practise." |

## Rules

- **Sentence case everywhere**: titles, buttons, tabs, eyebrows. Never use all caps.
- **Verb-first buttons** that say what happens: "Allow microphone", "Try it", "Keep talking", "Practise (3 min)". Avoid "OK", "Submit" and "Continue" when a specific verb exists.
- **Specific beats nice.** "You said 'actually' 4 times, down from 11" beats "Great progress!" There are no exclamation marks in Coach copy.
- **Name sounds the way people say them**: "the v sound", "v as in *van*". A mouth tip is one line: "Top teeth touch your lower lip for v."
- **Never describe an accent as a problem.** We fix clarity, not Bangladeshi-ness.
- **No guilt.** After a break the Buddy says "Welcome back," never "You missed 4 days." A reset streak reads "Let's start a new run."
- **Numbers only where they help.** Use counts the learner can feel ("× 11", "40 seconds", "1 of 3"), never scores or percentages. Skill levels are words: Building, Good, Strong.
- **No emoji** in UI or notifications. Warmth comes from the words.

## Banned words

These never appear in learner-facing copy (R6, R9):

> wrong · bad · error · mistake · fail · failed · incorrect · poor · phoneme · schwa · fricative · labiodental · accent problem · native-like

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| "Wrong pronunciation" | "Let's try once more" |
| "Error: mic denied" | "Bolo needs your mic to hear you" |
| "Session failed" | "We couldn't prepare your feedback. Your talk is saved." |
| "Labiodental fricative /v/" | "The v sound" |
| "You made 3 mistakes" | "3 things to try" |

## Bangla helper text

- Bangla appears under English, never instead of it, on onboarding and error screens (ONB-6) and on any tip after tapping "বাংলায়" (DBF-7).
- A native Bangla copywriter writes it and 3 target learners review it. Never ship machine translation alone. The Bangla strings in these previews are placeholders.
- Use everyday spoken Bangla (চলিত ভাষা), not textbook register. Keep English loanwords people actually use: "মাইক", "ইন্টারনেট".

## Notifications

- Notifications continue the Buddy's story in the third person, because they arrive from the app: "Bolo wants to know how the demo went." Inside the app, where the Buddy is on screen, it speaks for itself: "Did the payment fix go live? Tell me." That's one per day at most, and none if the learner already practised.
- In discreet mode the text is neutral: "Your session is ready." Notification text never names English, speaking or a course.
